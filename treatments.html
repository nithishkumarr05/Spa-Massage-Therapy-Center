Relaxation Massage

A full-body Swedish-style massage designed to quiet the nervous system and release everyday tension. Long, flowing strokes, gentle kneading, and warm oil combine to lower cortisol, improve circulation, and leave you floating.

Ideal for first-timers, chronic stress, poor sleep, or anyone who simply needs to switch off. Your therapist adjusts pressure to your comfort — from featherlight to firm.

Aromatherapy Massage

Where touch meets scent. Therapeutic-grade essential oils are blended specifically for your needs — whether that's calming lavender for anxiety, invigorating eucalyptus for fatigue, or balancing geranium for hormonal support.

The massage itself is gentle-to-medium pressure, allowing the oils to absorb while their aromatics work on the limbic system. You leave smelling faintly of the blend you chose — and feeling deeply settled.

Hot Stone Therapy

Smooth, heated basalt stones are placed along the spine and used as extension of the therapist's hands. The penetrating warmth relaxes muscles so deeply that the therapist can work at a gentler pressure while achieving greater release.

Perfect for cold weather, chronic stiffness, poor circulation, or anyone who finds traditional deep tissue too intense. The heat also calms the nervous system — many clients fall asleep within minutes.

TREATMENT BENEFITS

What Massage Actually Does

Beyond relaxation, regular massage therapy delivers measurable, science-backed benefits for both body and mind.

Lowers Blood Pressure

Regular sessions help reduce systolic and diastolic pressure by calming the nervous system and improving circulation.

Reduces Anxiety

Massage lowers cortisol and boosts serotonin and dopamine — measurable improvements in mood and emotional resilience.

Improves Sleep Quality

Studies show massage increases slow-wave sleep and reduces nighttime awakenings — often within a single session.

Speeds Muscle Recovery

Increased blood flow delivers oxygen and nutrients to tired muscles, reducing DOMS and accelerating repair after training.

Eases Chronic Pain

Deep tissue and trigger point work break the cycle of tension, inflammation, and referred pain in back, neck, and shoulders.

Boosts Immunity

Regular massage increases lymphocyte count and natural killer cell activity — your body's first line of defense.

Restores Mobility

Releasing fascial restrictions and muscular knots improves joint range of motion and everyday ease of movement.

Elevates Mood

Human touch triggers oxytocin release — the "bonding hormone" — creating feelings of safety, connection, and calm.

Slows Aging

Reduced stress hormones and improved circulation support collagen production, skin tone, and cellular repair.

Benefits are supported by peer-reviewed research in complementary therapies and clinical massage journals.
